Medicine runs short in one hospital while it sits unused in another.

Pharmacists still phone around to find stock. MediLink turns the search into a request, the answer into a plan, and the delivery into a tracked journey.

WORKFLOW ENGINE

How it works

  1. 01

    Monitor

    Every batch, by brand, manufacturer, expiry and storage condition, checked against treatments already booked.

    PHARMACY SHELF TELEMETRY
  2. 02

    Request

    A shortfall becomes a ready-to-send request, reviewed and sent by the pharmacist.

    ONE-TAP VERIFICATION
  3. 03

    Accept

    The nearest hospital with real surplus sees the request and accepts. The stock is reserved instantly.

    PEER ROUTING & MATCH
  4. 04

    Track

    A delivery partner carries it in a controlled environment while the receiving hospital watches location and temperature live.

    CHAIN-OF-CUSTODY LIVE
