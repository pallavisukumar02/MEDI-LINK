# Seed data: ALL SYNTHETIC

Every CSV in this folder is generated by `seed/generate.js` (run `npm run seed` at the repo root).

- Hospital names, stock, batches, brands, manufacturers, licences and patients are **fictional**.
- Hospital coordinates are real Bengaluru localities (OpenStreetMap); nothing else about them is real.
- Drugs follow NLEM 2022 section 8 (anticancer medicines); unit costs are illustrative.
- Patient refs are SHA-256 hashes of fake MRNs. No names exist anywhere in MediLink.
- Dates are relative to the anchor date in `seed/.anchor`, so "the next 14 days" always has bookings.

Demo fixtures that make every rule fire: flagged lot `ZX24K07` (H3, spurious), expired hospital licence (H4, Hebbal),
expired partner licence (P3), near-expiry surplus (H5 / H2), unsealed lot (H2-CARB-D).
Stock and expiry alerts: per-drug default reorder levels (`Drugs.reorderLevel`), hospital levels in `StockThresholds`;
H5 has an out-of-stock drug with an expired lot, a low drug and lots expiring in 85 / 25 / 5 days; H1 (after connecting) an
out-of-stock drug with an expired lot and a low drug.
